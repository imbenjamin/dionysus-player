## Description

Dionysus is a native iPhone and iPad client for Jellyfin, the free and open-source media server. It's built from the ground up for a fast, modern, fully native feel: no web wrapper, no ads, no subscriptions, no account beyond the Jellyfin server you already run.

REAL HDR PLAYBACK
Dionysus decodes Dolby Vision, HDR10 and HDR10+ video. It is not a downscaled or tone-mapped approximation. When your server and network support it, you get the same picture quality as the original file, untouched.

DOWNLOADS THAT ACTUALLY WORK OFFLINE
Download a movie, a single episode or a whole season, with a quality ladder you control: from full resolution down to a smaller file that saves storage and cellular data. Downloads continue in the background, browse fully offline, and sync your progress back when you reconnect.

MADE FOR IPHONE DUO
Open the phone and a title's page becomes a two-page spread. Stand it half folded on a table and the picture sits above the fold, the controls below. Close it and you carry on from the outer display, right where you left off.

DOLBY ATMOS AND DTS:X, HEIGHTS INCLUDED
Dionysus can decode the height channels of Atmos and DTS:X films, instead of a flat 5.1 mix, and play them as Spatial Audio on AirPods and over AirPlay. Turn it on in Profile → Spatial Audio (iOS 26 or later).

SMART, SERVER-AWARE STREAMING
The app negotiates with your Jellyfin server every time you press play. It uses Direct Play when your device can handle the source natively, or a transcode when it can't. Or force Direct Play if you'd rather control it yourself.

BUILT FOR YOUR SETUP
• Finds your server on your home network; sign in with Quick Connect
• Picture in Picture, and Now Playing on the Lock Screen
• Scrubbing previews, chapters, Skip Intro and Up Next
• Styled ASS/SSA subtitles and multi-track audio
• Playlists, box sets and full show pages
• VoiceOver throughout, and iPad layouts made for the larger screen

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Dionysus only ever talks to the Jellyfin server you configure. There's no analytics SDK, no ad network and no third-party service in the loop. Your watch history and credentials stay between your device and your server.

REQUIRES A JELLYFIN SERVER
This app is a client, not a media library. You'll need a Jellyfin server to connect to (free, open-source and self-hosted). If you don't have one yet, you can try Dionysus right away against Jellyfin's public demo server. You are responsible for hosting your Jellyfin server and content, and for meeting the copyright rules that apply to you.
